# 🛡️ Cybersecurity Architect | Defensive Security Specialist

Hi there! 👋<br><br>
I'm Rafi, a dedicated **Cybersecurity Architect** focused on designing, implementing, and optimizing layered security architectures. I leverage my background in systems engineering and Python to build resilient defenses that proactively mitigate risk and protect critical data.

My work centers on two key areas:

### Security Architecture & Design
I specialize in creating robust security blueprints, focusing on the strategic placement of controls. This includes implementing security best practices across the infrastructure lifecycle, ensuring **Security by Design** is the foundation for all systems.

### Defensive Security Operations
I have hands-on experience hardening systems and establishing continuous monitoring. My expertise spans:
* **Zero Trust & IAM:** Architecting identity and access management solutions and implementing Zero Trust principles for continuous verification.
* **Threat Detection:** Configuring and optimizing SIEM/logging platforms for enhanced visibility, correlation, and effective incident alerting.
* **Risk Mitigation:** Conducting vulnerability assessments and aligning security controls with established frameworks (e.g., NIST, ISO 27001).

I am always eager to explore emerging security technologies and strengthen the digital defense landscape. Feel free to explore my security-focused repositories, and don’t hesitate to reach out if you'd like to discuss architectural challenges or defensive strategies!
